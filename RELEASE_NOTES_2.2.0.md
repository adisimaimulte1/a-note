# A-Note 2.2.0

A-Note 2.2 focuses on smoother handwriting, more natural editing across pages, and improved reliability during everyday note-taking.

### What's new

- **Smoother handwriting** — reduced delayed stroke rendering during long writing sessions.
- **Cross-page tools** — continue writing, erasing, selecting and moving content across page boundaries.
- **Improved selection movement** — selections update naturally while moving between pages, including their controls and page-relative position.
- **Undoable movement** — moving selected handwriting is now included in Undo and Redo history.
- **More reliable erasing** — moved and restored strokes remain erasable, with more accurate highlighter deletion.
- **Responsive page navigation** — restored reliable page spacing, creation, scrolling and deletion behavior, including correct loading when jumping through long notebooks.
- **Safer page deletion** — confirmation dialogs identify the page number before deleting or clearing it.
- **Persistent drawing controls** — the bottom tool menu remains visible while writing.
- **Improved notebook opening** — additional safeguards prevent intermittent crashes when opening notebooks.
- **Crash diagnostics** — unexpected failures are recorded locally so logs can be reviewed later without telemetry.
- **Stability and performance improvements** throughout the editor.

Still fully local-first. No accounts, cloud dependency or telemetry.
